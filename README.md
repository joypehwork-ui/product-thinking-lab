# Product Thinking Lab

A self-contained interactive lesson on product thinking.

**▶ [Open it in your browser](https://joypehwork-ui.github.io/product-thinking-lab/)**

Or work offline: download `index.html` and double-click it. One file, no install, no internet needed.

## What's in it

Seven modules, following the structure of the [IDG Product Thinking pathway](https://www.idg.gov.sg/product-thinking/). Each one is short reading followed by something you actually have to do.

| # | Module | What you do |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Understanding the Problem | Judge four project requests: real problem, or solution in disguise? |
| 2 | Craft a Clear Problem Statement | Compare a weak and a strong statement, then write your own in the **4Cs** and get it checked |
| 3 | Start With The Whys | Walk a five-step root cause chain, then run one on your own problem |
| 4 | Metrics | Sort measures into output / adoption / outcome / guardrail |
| 5 | Assumptions and Risks | Place five assumptions on an impact × evidence map to find the riskiest |
| 6 | A Good Customer Experience | Read a full applicant journey and find where it actually breaks |
| 7 | Key Takeaways & Final Quiz | Ten questions across everything above |

## Notes

- **Progress and your drafts save automatically** in your browser's local storage (the 4Cs statement in Module 2 and the Five Whys chain in Module 3 are yours to keep). "Reset all progress" in the sidebar clears them.
- Every answer gets an explanation, including the wrong ones — those are where the actual teaching is, so it's worth deliberately picking a wrong answer occasionally.
- Worked examples are illustrative public-sector scenarios, not real cases.

## Suggested way through it

Do modules 1–7 in order once (about 30–40 minutes). Then come back to Modules 2 and 4 with a live project on your desk and fill them in for real — a problem statement you can't write is a project you don't yet understand.
